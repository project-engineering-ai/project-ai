# Welcome to Project AI

We're launching our platform for AI-powered information security. This is where cybersecurity meets artificial intelligence.

## What You'll Find Here

- **Threat research** — analysis of emerging attack techniques
- **AI security** — protecting ML systems from adversaries
- **Automation guides** — practical SOAR and SOC automation
- **Tool reviews** — open source security tools we use and build

## The Mission

Security teams are overwhelmed. Alerts pile up, threats evolve faster than humans can respond, and skilled analysts are scarce. AI isn't a silver bullet — but it's a force multiplier.

We're building tools that help security teams work smarter, not harder.
