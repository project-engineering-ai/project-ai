# About Project AI

## Who We Are

Project AI is a cybersecurity company at the intersection of artificial intelligence and information security. We build intelligent systems that protect organizations from evolving digital threats.

## Our Mission

To democratize AI-powered security — making advanced threat detection and response accessible to organizations of all sizes.

## What We Do

We combine machine learning with deep security expertise to:

- **Detect** — identify threats that traditional tools miss
- **Analyze** — understand attack patterns and adversary behavior
- **Respond** — automate incident response at machine speed
- **Protect** — harden AI systems against adversarial attacks

## Our Approach

- **Research-driven** — we stay ahead of emerging threats
- **Open source** — we contribute to the security community
- **Practical** — solutions that work in production, not just in papers
