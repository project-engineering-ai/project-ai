# AI in Cybersecurity: Beyond the Hype

Artificial intelligence is transforming cybersecurity — but not always in the ways vendors promise.

## What AI Actually Does Well

- **Pattern recognition** — finding anomalies in massive datasets
- **Speed** — analyzing threats faster than human analysts
- **Scale** — monitoring thousands of endpoints simultaneously
- **Consistency** — never gets tired, never misses a shift

## Where AI Falls Short

- **Context** — understanding business impact and risk
- **Creativity** — anticipating novel attack techniques
- **Explanation** — black-box models don't explain their reasoning
- **Adversarial robustness** — AI systems can be fooled

## The Real Opportunity

The best security operations combine human expertise with AI augmentation:

1. AI handles the volume — triaging alerts, enriching data, automating routine tasks
2. Humans handle the nuance — investigating complex threats, making risk decisions, improving defenses

## Getting Started

Don't buy the hype. Start with a specific problem:
- Too many false positives?
- Slow incident response?
- Alert fatigue?

Then find or build AI tools that solve that specific problem.
